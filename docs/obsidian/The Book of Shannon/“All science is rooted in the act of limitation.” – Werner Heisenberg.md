## 🧬 Interpretation

Heisenberg exposes the hidden engine behind science:

> To know anything precisely, you must **cut away** everything else.

Science is not the study of everything.  
It is the deliberate study of **one thing**, under **controlled constraints**.

- Every law emerges by **limiting variables**  
- Every equation begins with **what to exclude**
- Every experiment carves a **system boundary**

Limitation is not failure.  
It is the **structure that makes inquiry possible**.
