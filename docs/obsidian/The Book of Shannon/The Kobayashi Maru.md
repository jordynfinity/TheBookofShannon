An explanation of The Kobayashi Maru. 

Put simply. 

The Kobayashi Maru is "An Unwinnable Game" and "A Lesson in How to Accept the Inevitability of Failure" 

("To experience fear" (star trek 2009) is....misleading)

But if that's all it was. I would have simply said "This game is unwinnable" 

But that's not the point of the Maru. 

The point is "What will you try?" 

And most importantly "How will you win on the road to failure?" 

And really, the reason it's how Command officers are tested is "How does your humanity change the simulation?"
